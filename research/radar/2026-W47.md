# Radar, week 47 (16 to 22 Nov 2026)

The week's signal for me: German-language benchmarks are no longer empty ground, so my benchmark has to be narrower and sharper. And routing should only be trusted per route, measured on real inputs. Both point straight at Persona and the benchmark.

## Adopt

- **Measure routing per route, on my own inputs.** [EvalShift, 7 Nov](https://www.evalshift.dev/blog/llm-router) (a vendor post, so read with that in mind) argues that the savings are simple arithmetic but quality loss only shows per route. Their method: sample 100 to 300 real inputs, set a pass bar per route before the run, run both models on every case, and track misroutes plus missed savings. **For Persona:** this is almost exactly the eval set it needs. It also warns that a model switch breaks prompt caching, which belongs on the receipt as a cost line.

## Trial

- **Judge agreement as a published number.** [BenGER](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1266/) (EMNLP Findings, Oct 2026) benchmarks German legal reasoning with 596 case tasks and 531 short reasoning tasks. It checks its LLM judge against three blind human reviews and reports Pearson r 0.76 and Cohen's κ 0.60. **For the benchmark:** copy this. Hand-label a sample and publish how often my claim checker agrees with me.

## Assess

- **MÖVE, a German public-sector benchmark.** [arXiv 2606.13111](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13111) (June 2026) evaluates 39 models on summarization, question answering and topic extraction across ten German datasets. It also scores governance: hallucination, energy and transparency. Its headline is that no model wins every task and size predicts quality badly. **For the benchmark:** this overlaps with my idea. See the gap note [`gaps/2026-11-22-german-claim-checks.md`](../gaps/2026-11-22-german-claim-checks.md).
- **German Language LLM Index.** [dach.peerbench.ai](https://dach.peerbench.ai/) ranks 55 models on six German benchmarks, with cost per 1,000 questions and latency, updated 16 Oct 2026. **For Persona:** a quick way to shortlist which cheap model to test on German tasks first.
- **Agent evals move from outputs to actions.** In the [week's agent-eval news](https://dev.to/cognitalk/recent-news-in-agent-evaluation-nov-11-21-2b58), Langfuse deprecated its v4 trace-level LLM-as-judge evaluator (16 Nov), and Arena published an Agent Arena Index (18 Nov). **For Persona:** score tool calls and steps, not only the final text.

## Hold

- **Treating the EU high-risk delay as a general pause.** The [Digital Omnibus on AI](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-ai-omnibus-enters-force-amending-ai-act) entered into force on 27 Jul 2026. It moves high-risk deadlines to 2 Dec 2027 and 2 Aug 2028, but most Article 50 transparency duties already apply from 2 Aug 2026, with a watermarking grace period to 2 Dec 2026. **For me:** "show the working" is becoming a legal expectation as well as a design choice. That's worth one line in the Persona case study.
