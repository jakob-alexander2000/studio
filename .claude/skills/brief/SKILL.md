# Brief

1. Read the gap file and the radar it came from.
2. Fill `research/briefs/TEMPLATE.md`. Every claim gets a cited link you opened.
3. The smallest proof must fit in two weeks. If it doesn't, cut scope until it does.
4. "How I'll know" names the check that would prove the bet wrong, with a number.
5. Leave anything unknown as `[placeholder]` and list it as an open question at the end.
