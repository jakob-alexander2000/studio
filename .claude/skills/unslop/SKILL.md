# Unslop

## Never

- One italic serif word dropped into a sans headline.
- Inter, Geist, Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif or Bodoni as a default.
- Pill buttons, rounded cards, three-card icon grids, gradient blobs, grid-paper backgrounds, glassmorphism.
- A second accent colour, blue pencil marks, margin notes.
- Literal metaphors in copy: couture, tape tracks, gardens, atelier. Show the idea through the design instead.
- Words: delve, seamless, leverage, unlock, elevate, robust, cutting-edge, game-changer, journey, tapestry.
- Constructions: "not X, but Y", lists of three for rhythm, rhetorical questions as openers, em-dash chains, "In today's fast-paced world".
- Invented numbers, quotes, users or results.

## Always

- Concrete nouns and real numbers. Unknown means a visible `[placeholder]`, never a guess.
- First person, plain and friendly. Short sentences.
- Monochrome with red only for a failed check. Archivo and Martian Mono. Square corners. Visible construction.
- One hard gesture per page.

## Before and after

- Before: "I craft seamless AI experiences that empower teams." After: "I build AI tools that show their working."
- Before: a couture-themed page with "looks" and "fittings". After: the same projects as a plain numbered index.
