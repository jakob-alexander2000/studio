# Weekly radar

1. Read `research/sources.md`, `research/learning/syllabus.md`, the briefs in `research/briefs/` and last week's radar.
2. Go through each source for the past seven days. Open every page you take a fact from. A search snippet is not a source.
3. Keep only items that touch Jakob's work: evals and verification, model routing and cost, retrieval, German-language AI quality, EU AI rules, interfaces that show how an AI reached its answer, plus anything new and big.
4. Sort them into **Adopt** (use now), **Trial** (try on a project), **Assess** (watch), **Hold** (avoid, with why).
5. For each item write: one line on what it is, one line on why it matters *to Jakob*, naming the project, and the link.
6. Skip anything he already knows (check the syllabus and earlier radars) unless it changed.
7. Append counts to `research/signals.csv` (`week,topic,mentions,sources`). Flag a topic that grew three weeks in a row as **rising** and one that shrank as **fading**.
8. For each Trial or Assess item, answer the four gap questions in `research/gaps/TEMPLATE.md`. Save the strong ones to `research/gaps/`.
9. Add five recall questions from the week's material at the end.
10. Save as `research/radar/YYYY-Www.md`. Keep it to one page. Drop anything you can't cite.
